    Forges: Expensive armored combatants
        Gnome Engineer (places temporary turrets down), 
        Golem (tanky unit),

    The Bastion: Defensive faction
        Builder (builds walls and towers), 
        Archer (expensive unit that can stand on a wall for protection), 
        Mortar (slow-attacking, huge-range, small-AoE, medium-damage)

    The Cult: Swarms transitioning into hero units
        Cultists (extremely cheap, weak cannon fodder), 
        Cult Leader (expensive unit with weak stats that buffs nearby cultists),
        Elder God (expensive unit that can only be bought when 50 cultists are on the map; sacrifices cultists to heal or use abilities)
    
        Altar of Madness (provides income and reduces the number of cultists sacrificed per ability)
        Void Rift (attacks nearby enemies in melee with void tendrils)
    
    
    The Mycelium: Stalling with Mushlings and stealing units

    The Cryptlords: Slow unstoppable march

        Crypt (produces up to 6 skeletal archers to protect the castle)
    
    
    The Hostile Hospice:

        Toxic Smokestack (deals low damage in a very slowly growing AoE cloud)
    
    
    The Empire of Man: Weak units that get buffed when near units of the same type
        Hoplite (melee unit that gets more armor based on the number of nearby hoplites)
        Archer (ranged unit that gets a higher attack rate when near other archers)
        Bard (spellcaster that can give temporary shield HP to units; shields more with more bards around)
    
        Farm (produces income, which very slowly increases the longer the building survives)
        Archer Tower (fires arrows to damage enemies)
    
    
    Goblin Gang: Hit-and-run faction of skirmishing cavalry and infantry

        Pitfall Trap (damages units that get too close)

        Pestilence (applies a DOT in melee combat that has a chance to spread if that unit dies)
        War (powerful melee combatant)
        Famine (melee combatant that weakens all nearby enemies)
        Death (slow unit with extreme melee damage)
    
        Cairn (provides income; built when a horseman dies)
        The Marble Monument (reduces all enemy buildings’ max HP by 10%; built if Pestilence is alive)
        The Crimson Cenotaph (reduces all enemy units’ HP by 10%; built if War is alive)
        The Onyx Obelisk (reduces all enemy units’ damage by 10%; built if Famine is alive)
        The Alabaster Angel (1 enemy unit dies every 5 seconds; built if Death is alive)
    
    Celestial Symphony: A faction of expensive, weak units that give buffs
        Axe-Guitar (single-target melee unit that buffs damage)
        Tuba-Cannon (slow-attacking ranged unit that buffs health)
        War-Drummer (small, weak melee AoE unit that buffs movement speed)

Mechanics:
    Regions: Many battles happen simultaneously, and the winners are champions of regions (gaining a victor buff). The majority of regions on the world map determines planet ownership, and majorities of planets in a system determine solar system ownership. Majority solar system ownership at the end of the day determines that day’s victor.
        Battle maps,
        World maps,
        Solar system maps,
        Galactic map

    Hero units:
        Units gain experience based on the HP of slain enemy units. If they gain more XP than their max HP, they level up and get a 5% buff to all stats per level. At level 5, they gain a name and a hero ability.
    
    Weather:
        Occurs every 5 minutes.
        Frog Rain (hostile frogs fall all over the map)
        Blood Rain (heals all units)
        Wildfire (sets units inside on fire with a fire DOT)
        Smog (damages units inside the cloud slowly)
        Pestilence (gives 10 random units the Pestilence DOT, which has a chance to spread if they die)
        Blizzard (slows all units on the map)
        Earthquake (damages non-castle buildings)
        Madness (random units go insane and begin to attack friend and foe)
        Mania (all units stop fighting until the event ends)
        Midas Rain (all factions gain gold)
        Warp Storm (units randomly trade places)
        Meteor Storm (5 random locations are struck by high-damage meteors)
        Blessings (10 random units gain powerful buffs)
        Divine Wrath (the two units with the most health are killed)
